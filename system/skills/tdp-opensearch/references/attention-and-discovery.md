# Selecting follow-up observations

Attention is a priority queue. A selected count or display cap limits review,
not source coverage. Preserve underlying artifacts and report exclusions or
missing sources.

Group through source-linked feature, proteoform, PrSM and scan relations.
Compatible terminal frames, precursor identity, RT and acquisition can support
association; similar mass labels alone cannot. Same-interval ladders,
same-shift recurrence and homology families constrain different questions.

Useful priorities include relevant user/biological questions, repeat support,
localization uncertainty, coherent families, frame ambiguity and alternatives
that an available measurement could separate. These are considerations, not a
required checklist or fixed chemistry list. A high-quality shared match need
not be the most discriminating scan.

## Read the reported evidence fields

The ingest bundle preserves Smith ambiguity levels/categories, MS2 support
(`yes`, `partial`, `none`) and routing attention tiers. These fields describe
different properties; they are not interchangeable confidence scores. Read
qualification thresholds from the actual output. Automatic reference matches
do not remove identity or localization ambiguity from the source grade.
Q-values and E-values also have different semantics; missing coverage cannot
be inferred from fragment count.

Continue a selected question with its observations, source frames and existing
artifacts. A later tranche can reuse the inventory. Additional chemistry
construction and targeted measurements belong to Copilot, not batch census.
