# Human decisions

Only an explicit human verdict creates a decision record. Testing a hypothesis,
a leaning, and silence are not decisions.

Keep one short `decision.md` beside the current question's Run-local artifacts;
reuse it and mark later revisions. Record the question and source IDs, the
user's decision and message/date reference, their stated reason (or "not
stated"), evidence/knowledge links, and remaining uncertainty. Link it in chat.
Do not duplicate spectra or the full hypothesis summary.

Index that same card in the workspace wiki, attributed to the human, with the
anchor, competing models, discriminating evidence and what decided it. The
card is the case's narrative record; the wiki points to it rather than storing
a second report. System-wiki promotion uses the `evolution` skill and its
evidence requirements. Never inject case answers into unrelated analyses.
