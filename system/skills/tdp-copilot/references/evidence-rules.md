# Interpreting evidence

Shared claim boundaries live in scientific_honesty. Use this reference for
mass-shift-specific interpretation.

| Source | Can support | Does not establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| MS1 registration and mass closure | Mass/composition compatibility | Identity or site |
| RT-coherent family | Recurrence and shared-increment hypothesis | Chemistry transfer between members |
| Comparable MS1 envelopes | Composition discrimination | Localization |
| Activation-appropriate fragments | Backbone/model/site discrimination | Biological function |
| Neutral loss or diagnostic ion | A compatible chemical group | Residue site |
| Reference annotation, motif or memory | Candidate and biological prior | Its presence in this sample |

Interpret a measurement against the credible alternatives actually tested.
Report co-isolation, calibration problems and scan disagreements. Aggregate
fragment counts or coverage cannot replace the local ions that distinguish
models. An untested alternative remains untested.

Keep residuals signed and tied to their source frame, uncertainty and accounted
components. Distinguish full-chain, processed-chain and fragment coordinates.
Negative evidence states what was searched and the detection limits.

SUPPORTS, REFUTES and DISCRIMINATES describe specific source-linked comparisons.
They do not automatically assign final chemistry; explained remains an
attributed human verdict. An unresolved comparison can still answer the task.

## Reported grades

Smith proteoform level describes ambiguity in gene, sequence, PTM identity and
localization. E-value describes spectrum-match quality. Use the shared
classifier when a new level is needed and its inputs are available; keep the
underlying assumptions visible. More measurements or a higher score do not by
themselves improve a definition level.
